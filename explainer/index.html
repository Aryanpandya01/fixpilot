FixPilot: AnyDesk, but the AI fixes it

The customer describes the problem. FixPilot looks at their screen, finds the right buttons, and fixes it or shows them how. No human support agent, no waiting.

01 · The problem

Today a human fixes every issue by hand

Software vendors answer the same questions all day: "where is this menu", "how do I add a product", "my icon is missing". Every one ends with an AnyDesk session.

02 · Two ways to use it

One app, two modes

Mode 1 · Personal help

Anyone with a Windows PC

"My WiFi and Bluetooth icons are gone." "How do I make a new folder?"

  • Type or speak the problem
  • Watch FixPilot fix it on your screen
  • Uses Gemini's general Windows knowledge

Free. This is how people discover FixPilot.

Mode 2 · Business support

Customers of a software company

A billing software customer asks support: "How do I add a new product with 18% GST?"

  • Support creates a 6-digit FixPilot code
  • Customer enters the code and clicks Accept
  • AI solves it using that vendor's knowledge base

Paid by the vendor. This is the business.

04 · System design

What runs where

The AI runs on the customer's own PC, so there is no screen streaming. Only screenshots go to Gemini, and only step updates go to the support desk.

05 · The heart of the system

The AI loop: look, decide, check, act, repeat

06 · Trust

The customer chooses how much the AI does

Guide

AI points, you click

A glowing circle on the right button: "Click Products here". Best for learning.

Assist

AI clicks after you approve

Every step shows Approve / Deny first.

Auto

AI fixes it alone

Only for safe, known fixes the vendor allows.

Safety built in

Code + Accept before anything STOP button + Ctrl+Shift+X Gemini safety checks on risky steps No deleting, no passwords, no payments Prompt-injection detection Every step logged Unsure → human support
