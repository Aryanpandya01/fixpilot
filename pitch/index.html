Slide 6 / 10 • Safety
Trust & Safety

Safety & Guardrails by Design

  • Explicit Consent: Ephemeral 6-digit session codes with mandatory Accept dialog.
  • 3 Operational Modes: Guide Mode (visual arrows only), Assist Mode (step-by-step confirmation), Auto Mode.
  • Global Kill Switch: Ctrl+Shift+X immediately ceases all automation.
  • Hard Boundaries: Never touches passwords, admin UAC popups, financial payments, or data deletion.
